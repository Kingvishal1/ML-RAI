V20 ONE-BALL CORRECTIVE LEARNING — EXPERIMENTAL

WHAT IT IS:
- Existing V4 grasp stays neural, original V20 base/arm approach stay neural.
- Existing 4+ verified teacher demonstrations are REUSED as supervised data.
  These OFFLINE teaching labels WERE SCRIPTED; no scripted expert motion is
  executed in clean TEST mode. Strict script-free training is NOT achieved.
- During correction collection the student controls the arm; a separate
  counterfactual IK oracle LABELS those visited states for training, but its
  actions are NOT sent to motors. Therefore, the learner has real failure-state
  examples, which ordinary behavioral cloning lacked.
- In clean TEST mode the correction-label oracle is disabled. Arm bend/lower
  joint targets and release decisions are generated by network predictions.
- For this LOCAL DROP experiment, expert carry is DISABLED and no wheel
  travel is commanded after the grasp: ball is dropped near the pickup XY.
  This is NOT the original distant-circle pick-and-place task. The existing
  base/arm approach and V4 gripper are learned. Phase switching, physics
  checks and actuator servos remain programmed infrastructure.
- Robot simulation is NOT executable in this container; physical test is unverified.

INSTALL: Extract over your existing robotnik_arm_task directory.
Prior training assets required on your laptop:
  trained_v20_short_bend_release/release_student.npz
  v20_short_bend_demos/expert_*.npz (4+ verified demos)

COMMANDS:
  1. bash RUN_COLLECT_STUDENT_CORRECTIONS.sh
     Requires reaching the lowering stage. Even a failed student run can
     yield counterfactual labels; no expert action is applied to the robot.
  2. bash RUN_RETRAIN_CORRECTIVE.sh
  3. bash RUN_TEST_CORRECTIVE.sh
  4. grep -E 'V4_GRAVITY_HOLD_PASS|V20_RELEASE_STUDENT_ACTION|V20_CIRCULAR_DROP_RESULT|V9_GROUND_PLACE_ABORT' v20_corrective_test.log | tail -30

The trained release may still fail: at least one complete closed-loop physical
PASS is needed. This is imitation learning WITH scripted offline labels;
not training without any scripts whatsoever. No hidden expert fallback in
student arm lowering or finger opening.

DROP ZONE: local circle center (2.47,3.03), radius 0.32m. Ball pickup at (2.47,3.03,1).


10-GRAM PHYSICS PATCH (2026-11-04):
- Both the teacher data-collection runtime and neural student runtime now use
  ball_mass = 0.010 kg (10 g), including the gravity probe and dynamic ball.
- The ball still falls under gravity; no virtual attachment or antigravity force.
- Older 50 g demonstrations/checkpoints are NOT equivalent to 10 g training.
  To obtain 10 g-specific demonstrations, archive the OLD directory and run:
    mv v20_short_bend_demos v20_short_bend_demos_50g_backup
    bash RUN_TRAIN_SHORT_BEND.sh
  This produces a new 10 g short-bend release checkpoint.
- To collect corrective rollouts for 10 g, use the newly trained checkpoint
  and follow RUN_COLLECT_STUDENT_CORRECTIONS.sh -> RUN_RETRAIN_CORRECTIVE.sh
  -> RUN_TEST_CORRECTIVE.sh.
- A physics change alone is NOT evidence that the policy can physically
  complete grasp, bend and release. Test remains necessary.
